# Mechanics Governance

Read `/CONTRACT.md` first.

How a change to the mechanics layer is proposed. Active governance is the implemented content
of `/CONTRACT.md`, `/RULES.md`, the governance schemas and templates, the bootstrap files and
the preflight validator; nothing in this folder is active by being stored here (CONTRACT §13.2).

This repository carries no decision history. An owner's own record of how they accepted and
applied governance changes – including changes to these mechanics in their brain – lives in
their memory, at `/memory/governance/proposals/`.

#### Folders

##### `proposals/`

Explains how to propose a change to the mechanics and holds a proposal while it is under
review. Owner-layer proposals live in `/memory/governance/proposals/`.
